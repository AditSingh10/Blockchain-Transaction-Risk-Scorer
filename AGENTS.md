## Frontend workflow

- Before any substantial frontend implementation or redesign, invoke
  `$frontend-art-direction`.
- After meaningful visual changes, invoke `$browser-visual-qa`.
- Before handing off frontend work, invoke `$anti-generic-ui-critic`
  and correct every P0 and P1 issue.
- Do not call frontend work complete until the affected workflows have
  been rendered and visually inspected.
